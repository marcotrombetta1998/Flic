## What is FLIC?

FLIC is a mobile-first creator monetization platform where fans don't just watch content — they **interrogate it**. Every video uploaded to FLIC is processed by an AI pipeline that extracts structured knowledge: recipes, travel itineraries, workout programs, financial strategies, music chords, match statistics. Fans can then chat with the video directly, ask any question, and get precise answers — all powered by a gamified credit system that makes paying feel like winning.

## The Three Innovations

### 1. AI Layer on Every Video
Every video uploaded is automatically processed:
- **Transcription** (OpenAI Whisper) — full transcript with speaker identification
- **Visual Analysis** (GPT-4o Vision) — landmarks, products, food, people identified frame by frame
- **Structured Extraction** — vertical-specific data pulled out automatically:
  - Travel → hotels, restaurants, coordinates, costs, itinerary
  - Food → ingredients, quantities, steps, calories, substitutions
  - Fitness → exercises, sets, reps, rest times, muscle groups
  - Finance → tickers, allocations, strategies, risk levels
  - Music → chords, tabs, BPM, key, song structure
  - Sport → scores, goals, stats, player events with timestamps
- **Knowledge Graph** — all extracted entities indexed and queryable

### 2. Conversational Interface (The FLIC Bot)
Below every video, a chat interface lets fans interrogate content in natural language:

```
Fan: "Where did he stay in Medellín?"
Bot: "He stayed at the Selina El Poblado — about €45/night. 
      Great spot for digital nomads, rooftop bar included.
      Are you planning a Colombia trip too?"
      
      ✈️ Air Europa has direct flights Milan → Bogotá from €380 this month →
```

The bot:
- Answers from the video's specific knowledge graph
- Always follows up with an engagement question
- Injects contextually relevant commercial offers at the right moment
- Costs 1–3 credits per query depending on complexity

### 3. Cross-Creator Aggregation
FLIC's most defensible feature. The AI combines knowledge from multiple creators to generate composite experiences exclusive to the platform:

- 8 travel creators visited Tokyo → FLIC generates one unified itinerary from all 8 perspectives
- 5 fitness creators → FLIC generates a weekly program combining their workouts
- 12 recipe videos → FLIC generates a personalized meal plan with shopping list

This cross-creator intelligence belongs to FLIC. It compounds in value with every creator added. No competitor can replicate it without FLIC's scale.

## How Monetization Works

### For Users — The Credit System
Users buy credit packs (€5 / €10 / €20 / €50) and spend credits to unlock value:

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Watch a video | Free |
| Basic bot query | 1 credit |
| Complex query (itinerary, recipe) | 2–3 credits |
| Unlock full video details | 1–5 credits |
| Cross-creator composite content | 8 credits |
| Pinned comment (guaranteed creator reply) | 1 credit |

### Gamification — Paying Feels Like Winning
Every credit top-up triggers a gamification moment:

- **Spin Wheel** — spin after every purchase for 1.5×, 2×, or 3× credit multiplier
- **Mystery Drop** — €5 blind pack: AI selects content perfectly matched to your taste profile
- **Surge Inverse** — first 50 fans on any new video pay 50% fewer credits (opposite of surge pricing)
- **Challenge Rewards** — complete a 7-day fitness streak → earn 20 free credits
- **Fan Cards** — limited-edition creator collectibles (max 100 minted), resellable, grant exclusive perks

### For Creators — Five Revenue Streams
1. **Credit earnings** — share of every unlock, query, and pinned comment on their content
2. **Monthly subscriptions** — fans subscribe for full access to a creator's library
3. **1:1 consultations** — creator-set pricing, booked through integrated calendar
4. **Brand deal integration** — FLIC bot serves their sponsors contextually
5. **Fan card sales** — limited editions minted directly by creator

### Platform Revenue — The Contextual Commerce Engine
When a user asks the bot "where did he stay in Medellín?", FLIC knows:
- Destination intent
- Travel budget (from credit purchase history)
- Current timing

The bot answers and surfaces a precisely relevant commercial offer. Air Europa pays FLIC €18–45 per booking. This is not advertising — it is high-intent commerce at the exact moment of decision. Conversion rates project at 4–8× industry standard for display ads.

## Technical Architecture

### Frontend
- React Native + Expo SDK 51
- Expo Router (file-based navigation)
- Syne Bold + DM Sans + JetBrains Mono (typography)
- React Native Reanimated 3 + Moti (animations)
- Zustand (global state)
- Dark luxury aesthetic — #0A0A0A base, #00C878 lime accent

### Backend
- Node.js 20 + TypeScript + Fastify
- Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + Storage + Realtime)
- Drizzle ORM
- OpenAI GPT-4o + Whisper + Vision
- BullMQ + Redis (async video processing queue)
- Stripe (PSD2 compliant payments + Connect for creator payouts)
- Resend (transactional email)

### AI Pipeline (per ogni video uploadato)
```
Upload → Queue →
  Step 1: Whisper transcription + speaker ID
  Step 2: GPT-4o Vision frame analysis
  Step 3: Structured extraction (vertical-specific)
  Step 4: Bot system prompt generation
  Step 5: Thumbnail optimization
→ ai_processed = true → notify creator
```
